Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

The short version: this site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Your lesson progress is saved only in your own browser.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Claude Code Playbook website at claude-code-playbook.vercel.app (the "site"). It explains what information is involved when you visit and what happens to it.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details. There is nothing to sign up for, and the site has no contact or login forms. We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, social media trackers or tracking cookies.

Information stored in your browser

To remember your place, the site saves a small amount of data in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • ccp-progress-v1: which lessons you marked complete (and when), the days you studied (up to the last 60 days) and the last lesson you opened. This powers the progress ring, streak and "Up next" card.
  • ccp-theme: whether you chose the light or dark theme.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Private or incognito windows usually erase it when you close them.

Third-party services

Two outside services are involved when you load the site. Each processes a minimal amount of technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, which is needed to deliver content over the internet:

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web server, it may keep short-lived access logs for security and reliability. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on the page, so your browser requests font files from Google's servers. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ and the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to other websites

The guide links to external sites, such as official documentation. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies when you visit them.

Children

The site is a general-audience learning resource and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. The data in your browser's local storage is fully under your control, as described above. For data handled by Vercel or Google, you can use the rights described in their policies.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes, for example if analytics are added, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change.
